L5: REVIEW QUESTION
L6: In registered nurses working in clinical practice, do structured mindfulness-based interventions,
L7: compared with control conditions that do not involve mindfulness training, reduce burnout?

L9: ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA (a record must meet all of C1-C6 to be included)
L10: C1 Population. Registered nurses (RNs, including specialist and advanced-practice RNs) currently working
L11: in clinical practice in any setting. Mixed staff samples are eligible only if RNs make up at least 80%
L12: of participants OR results for RNs are reported separately. Not eligible: nursing students; nursing
L13: assistants or healthcare assistants on their own; physicians; other professions.
L14: C2 Intervention. A structured, multi-session (at least 4 sessions) mindfulness-based intervention whose
L15: core component is training in formal mindfulness meditation practice (e.g. MBSR, MBCT, or adaptations of
L16: them), delivered in person, online or via an app. Not eligible: single-session interventions; yoga, tai
L17: chi, progressive muscle relaxation, guided imagery, breathing exercises or general stress-management
L18: programmes that do not include formal mindfulness meditation training.
L19: C3 Comparator. Any control condition without mindfulness training: waitlist, usual care / no
L20: intervention, attention control, or an active non-mindfulness intervention.

L24: excluded because its burnout result was non-significant.
