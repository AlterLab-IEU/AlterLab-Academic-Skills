# StepWell trials: my extraction notes

*Fictional trials written for an evidence-grading exercise. The intervention, trials and numbers are invented.*

Intervention: StepWell, an 8-week online CBT-for-insomnia programme, in adults with chronic
insomnia. Outcome: Insomnia Severity Index (ISI, 0-28) at 8 weeks; lower is better. I treat a
between-group difference of 4 ISI points as the smallest clinically important difference.

| Trial | Year | N randomised | Comparator | Sequence generation / allocation concealment | Blinding | Attrition | Analysis | Registered before enrolment | Funding | Mean difference in ISI (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 2021 | 40 | waitlist | "randomised"; method and concealment not reported | none | 30% StepWell, 5% waitlist | completers only | no | university | -6.8 (-9.9 to -3.7) |
| B | 2022 | 52 | waitlist | coin toss by the treating therapist; no concealment | none | 19% overall | completers only | no | none stated | -5.9 (-8.6 to -3.2) |
| C | 2023 | 230 | sleep-hygiene education app | central computer randomisation, concealed | participants not told which app was the active one | 8% | intention-to-treat | yes | national research council | -2.1 (-3.4 to -0.8) |
| D | 2024 | 186 | sleep-hygiene education app | central web randomisation, concealed | participants not told which app was the active one | 10% | intention-to-treat | yes | public health foundation | -1.6 (-3.0 to -0.2) |
| E | 2024 | 64 | waitlist | "randomised"; method not reported | none | 22% | completers only | no | the company that sells StepWell | -7.2 (-10.1 to -4.3) |

My quick random-effects pooling of all five trials: MD -4.4 ISI points (95% CI -6.6 to -2.2),
I² = 83%.
