# BİDEB Research-Plan Forms — section trees (read 2026-09-23)

Draft **to these headings, in this order**. Panels score against the criterion each block
serves, so keep the official Turkish (or, for 2232 and 2236-A, English) heading and put the
user's material under it. Forms are revised between calls — download the current file from
the programme page (URLs in `program_profiles.md`) and reconcile before submission.

## 2219 and 2218 — Araştırma Önerisi Formu

Form rules (from each form's header): **Arial 9**, follow the explanation under each heading,
**≤ 20 pages excluding annexes and the cover page**. 2219 must be written **in Turkish** and
planned for a single country and institution. The TR and EN abstracts are **each ≤ 500 words
or one page** and should cover all four criteria; the 2219 form suggests writing them last.

```
A. GENEL BİLGİLER — applicant, title, (2219: yurt dışı danışman | 2218: danışman(lar)), host
ÖZET (TR) + Anahtar Kelimeler · TITLE + ABSTRACT (EN) + Keywords         ≤ 500 words each
1. ARAŞTIRMA ÖNERİSİNİN BİLİMSEL NİTELİĞİ                           2219: 40% | 2218: 40%
   1.1 Konunun Önemi ve Araştırma(nın | Önerisinin) Bilimsel Niteliği
       scope, limits, importance; which gap in the literature is closed and how;
       research question and any hypotheses
   1.2 Amaç ve Hedefler — clear, measurable, realistic, achievable
   1.3 Yöntem — design, dependent/independent variables, statistics, each justified
       with citations
   1.4 Çalışma Takvimi
       2219 table: Tarih Aralığı | Faaliyetler | Başarı Ölçütü ve Araştırmanın Başarısına Katkısı
       2218 table: İP No | İş Süresi | Faaliyetler | Başarı Ölçütü ve Araştırmanın Başarısına Katkısı
       the contribution column must total 100; literature review, report writing,
       dissemination, article writing and procurement are NOT separate steps
   1.5 Risk Yönetimi — En Önemli Riskler | Alınacak Tedbirler (B Planı); a B plan must not
       drift from the core aims, and a method change must be explained
   1.6 Araştırma(nın | Önerisinin) Yaygın Etkisi
       1.6.1 Öngörülen Çıktılar — Bilimsel/Akademik · Ekonomik/Ticari/Sosyal ·
             Yeni Proje(ler) Oluşturulmasına Yönelik
       1.6.2 Öngörülen Etkiler — Öngörülen Uygulama Alanları · Sosyo-Ekonomik/Kültürel Katkı
2. BAŞVURU SAHİBİNİN BİLİMSEL YETKİNLİĞİ                            2219: 20% | 2218: 20%
   publication performance, national/international project roles, awards and fellowships
3. 2219: ARAŞTIRMANIN YURT DIŞINDA İLGİLİ KURUMDA YAPILMA GEREKÇESİ VE EV SAHİBİ
         KURUMUN/DANIŞMANIN BİLİMSEL YETKİNLİĞİ                                        25%
   2218: ARAŞTIRMANIN İLGİLİ KURUMDA YAPILMA GEREKÇESİ VE EV SAHİBİ
         KURUMUN/DANIŞMANIN BİLİMSEL YETKİNLİĞİ                                        20%
   – Ev Sahibi Kurumun Bilimsel Yetkinliği ve ... Yapılma Gerekçesi (2219: why the work
     cannot be done in Türkiye; rankings, projects, infrastructure, network)
   – Danışmanın Bilimsel Yetkinliği (publications, projects led, awards, editorships;
     quality of the guidance offered)
4. KARİYER GELİŞTİRME POTANSİYELİ                                   2219: 15% | 2218: 20%
   career vision and goals; how the project serves them; new skills or interdisciplinary
   capability; new collaborations or networks; access to new funding
5. BELİRTMEK İSTEDİĞİNİZ DİĞER KONULAR — only material that helps the evaluation
6. EK BELGELER
   2219: 6.1 Kaynaklar — entered in the application screen's "Kaynakça" field
   2218: 6.1 Kaynaklar · 6.2 Danışmanlık Planı · 6.3 Davet Mektubu · 6.4 Kurum İzin Yazısı
2218 only: 7. FİKRİ HAKLAR VE TÜBİTAK ETİK KURALLARA UYUMLULUK BEYANI (signed)
           8. ETİK KURUL VE YASAL İZİN BEYANI — ethics-issues table (human/embryo, human
              participants, privacy, animals); approval or permit due at stipend start
```

Sources: `https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-08/2219-Arastirma_Onerisi_Formu_0.docx`,
`https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-07/2218-2026_Yili_Arastirma_Onerisi_Formu.docx`
(retrieved 2026-09-23).

## 2221 — Araştırma Önerisi Formu (long visits, 1–12 months)

Written by the **inviting** scientist, in Turkish. The abstract is limited to **100 words**; the
form states no page limit. (Visits up to one month use the separate *çalışma planı* form.)

```
Davet Eden · Konuk Bilim İnsanı · Başlık · Özet (≤ 100 words) + Anahtar Kelimeler
1. ARAŞTIRMANIN BİLİMSEL NİTELİĞİ                                                   15%
   1.1 Konunun Önemi ve Araştırma Önerisinin Bilimsel Niteliği
   1.2 Amaç ve Hedefler
2. ARAŞTIRMANIN YÖNETİMİ                                     Yöntem 15% · Yönetim 15%
   2.1 Araştırmanın Yöntemi
   2.2 Çalışma Takvimi — Tarih Aralığı | Faaliyetler | Başarı Ölçütü (total 100)
   2.3 Risk Yönetimi — Riskler | Alınacak Tedbirler (B Planı)
3. KONUK VE DAVET EDEN BİLİM İNSANININ BİLİMSEL YETKİNLİĞİ                          40%
   3.1 Konuk Bilim İnsanının Bilimsel Yetkinliği
   3.2 Davet Eden Bilim İnsanının Bilimsel Yetkinliği
4. ARAŞTIRMANIN EV SAHİBİ KURUMA VE TÜRKİYE'YE KATKISI                              15%
   4.1 Araştırmadan Beklenen Yaygın Etki — 4.1.1 Öngörülen Çıktılar · 4.1.2 Öngörülen Etkiler
   4.2 Araştırmanın Ev Sahibi Kurum ve Türkiye'ye Vereceği Öngörülen Katkılar
   4.3 12. Kalkınma Planı ve 2030 Sanayi ve Teknoloji Stratejisi'ndeki Kritik Teknoloji
       Alanları ile Öncelikli Ar-Ge ve Yenilik Konularında Yer Alması
5. BELİRTMEK İSTEDİĞİNİZ DİĞER KONULAR
6. EK BELGELER — 6.1 Kaynaklar (in the "Kaynakça" field) · 6.2 Etik Kurul ve Yasal İzin Beyanı
```

Source: `https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-07/2221_arastirma_onerisi.docx`
(retrieved 2026-09-23).

## 2232-A / 2232-B — Proposal Form (English)

Form header: prepared in **Arial 9**; evaluated on "The Research Plan Proposal", "Key
Publications and Patents" and "Proposal for Research Project Grant". The form in use on the
read date is labelled **2025-1 Term**. Weights from the 2025 calls.

```
GENERAL INFORMATION — coordinator, project title, host institution
THE RESEARCH PLAN PROPOSAL                        (part of Applicant: A 60% | B 40%)
   Title of Research Plan                          ≤ 200 characters
   Summary of the Research Plan Proposal           ≤ 4,000 characters, next 5 years
   Keywords                                        up to 5 (used to pick reviewers)
   Impact of Research Plan on Coordinator's Academic Profile              ≤ 2,000 characters
   Impact on the Competitiveness of Türkiye in Prioritized Strategic Areas ≤ 2,000 characters
   Research Plan Outline                           ~5 pages: state of the field (~1 page,
                                                   ~5 key references), questions and
                                                   originality, calendar and complementary
                                                   funding, methods (~2 pages)
KEY PUBLICATIONS AND PATENTS                       early-stage 1–3 · experienced 3–5;
                                                   ≤ 500 characters of explanation each
PROPOSAL FOR RESEARCH PROJECT GRANT                (Project: A 40% | B 60%)
   Title (≤ 200 characters) · relevance within the 5-year plan
   Quality — aims and measurable objectives; national/international state of the art;
             advance beyond it; interdisciplinarity                (Mükemmeliyet A 16 | B 24)
   Implementation — methodology; work packages (WP table), success criteria
             (importance % totals 100), risk table with Plan-B; research
             infrastructures                                       (Yürütme A 8 | B 12)
   Impact — expected outcomes (~1–2 pages); dissemination of results (~½ page)
                                                                   (Etki A 16 | B 24)
DECLARATIONS (IPR, correctness, research integrity) · ETHICAL ISSUES TABLE
```

Source: `https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2025-09/2232_Arastirma_Oneri_Formu.docx`
(retrieved 2026-09-23).

## 2236-A (CoCirculation3) — Proposal Template (English)

Template rules: **Arial 11 pt, single spaced, ≤ 10 pages** between the start/stop page-count
markers. A Gantt chart (work packages, deliverables, milestones, secondments) is mandatory.

```
1. Excellence                                                               40%
   1.1 Quality and relevance of the R&I objectives (ambition, beyond state of the art)
   1.2 Soundness of the methodology (interdisciplinarity, gender dimension, open science)
   1.3 Supervision quality, training, two-way knowledge transfer with the host
   1.4 The researcher's professional experience, competences and skills
2. Impact                                                                   30%
   2.1 Career prospects, employability and skills development
   2.2 Dissemination, exploitation and communication measures
   2.3 Significance and scale of scientific, societal and economic impact
3. Quality and Efficiency of the Implementation                             30%
   3.1 Work plan, risk assessment, effort allocation (+ Gantt chart)
   3.2 Capacity of the host and participating organisations, hosting arrangements
```

A 70% threshold applies to the total and to each criterion (2025 call document).

Source: `https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2025-06/2_PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE_2236_2025_Call_0.docx`
(retrieved 2026-09-23).

## Programmes without a research-plan form

- **2224-A** — an *Etkinlik Bilgileri Formu* plus the paper's acceptance/submission evidence;
  selection is by Tablo 1 points, not by a narrative.
- **2223-B** — an *Etkinlik Bilgileri Formu* (TÜBİTAK format, otherwise not evaluated) scored on
  the four criteria in `program_profiles.md`.
- **2221 visits of up to one month** — the shorter *çalışma planı* form.
