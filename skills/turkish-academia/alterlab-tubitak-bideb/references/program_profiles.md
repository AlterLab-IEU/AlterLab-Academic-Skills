# BİDEB Programme Profiles — verified, sourced, dated

**Every figure on this page was read from the live tubitak.gov.tr programme page and/or the
call document linked from it on 2026-09-23.** Amounts, dates and eligibility rules change with
each call. Before quoting any figure to a user, WebFetch the programme page in the Sources line
of that section and reconcile. Where a page gives no date for an amount, the table says so
rather than guessing one.

Abbreviations: **GYK** = Grup Yürütme Kurulu (the BİDEB group executive board that decides);
**TYBS** = TÜBİTAK Yönetim Bilgi Sistemi (`tybs.tubitak.gov.tr`, the application system);
**e-BİDEB** = BİDEB Başvuru ve İzleme Sistemi (`ebideb.tubitak.gov.tr`); **PTS** = BİDEB Proje
Takip Sistemi (`bideb-pts.tubitak.gov.tr`); **ARBİS** = Araştırmacı Bilgi Sistemi
(`arbis.tubitak.gov.tr`); **Tablo 1** = the publication-point table printed in each call.

## 2219 — Yurt Dışı Doktora Sonrası Araştırma Burs Programı

Postdoctoral research **abroad**, at one host institution in one country.

| Item | Verified content (2026 call) |
|------|------------------------------|
| Purpose (call §1) | Support scientists "araştırmacıların bilimsel üretkenliğini artırmak, uluslararası işbirliklerini güçlendirmek ve alanlarında yenilikçi yöntem ve yaklaşımlar edinerek akademik yetkinliklerini geliştirmelerini sağlamak amacıyla" |
| Who can apply (§4.1) | T.C. citizen · doctorate, or specialty in medicine/dentistry/pharmacy/veterinary, or sanatta yeterlik · has not used 2219 within the six years since returning from a previous 2219 · invited by a doctorate-holding academic at a host that, **in the applicant's field and in the application year**, is in the QS or THE top 100 (universities) or Scimago Institutions Rankings–Government top 250 (public research institutes) — **or** by a host academic whose CNCI or FWCI is ≥ 1.00 (all publications, all years) · YDS / e-YDS / YÖKDİL / e-YÖKDİL / TIPDİL ≥ 65 (or an ÖSYM-accepted equivalent) **or** a degree from a university teaching 100% in a foreign language · not employed at an institution abroad · not retired · doctorate not from the host · previous-period TÜBİMER objection process completed · TÜBİTAK Başkanlık staff cannot apply (§9.1.9) |
| Time since PhD / age | The 2026 call's §4.1 list contains **no** time-since-doctorate or age limit |
| What it pays | Monthly stipend **up to 2,350 EUR (European countries), 2,050 GBP (United Kingdom), 2,600 USD (other countries)**; per-country amounts in `2219_ulkelere_gore_burs_miktarlari_2026.pdf`. One-off round-trip flight for **at most 3 people including the fellow**, in one of four combinations listed on the page (e.g. fellow + spouse + one child under 18). Paid monthly into a foreign-currency account in Türkiye or abroad |
| Duration | ≤ 12 months, as approved by TÜBİTAK. Extensions: total ≤ 24 months (12 with stipend + 12 without); a paid extension is considered only for force majeure (§6.5.1) |
| Calendar | Annual call. **2026 call: 20 Jul 2026 – 15 Sep 2026, 17:30** (closed on the read date) |
| System | TYBS → *Başvuru Yönetimi / Açık Çağrılar*; editable until the deadline; afterwards visible in e-BİDEB *Önceki Başvurularım* |
| Documents (§4.2 + page) | Research proposal on the TÜBİTAK form (see `form_structures.md`) · doctorate / sanatta yeterlik diploma or Ministry-approved specialty certificate · invitation letter (host letterhead; valid dates as month/year; topic/title; **research language**; signed by the host advisor or an institutional official, in which case the advisor's details must appear; no library invitations; conditional letters only with proof the condition is met; certified TR/EN translation for other languages; one country, one institution, one letter) · employer permission letter if employed (signed by the rector or minister, or on their behalf) — or proof of not being employed · detailed CV with the ARBİS publication list · evidence for the host-ranking or advisor-impact condition (per the *Kurum Sıralama ve Danışman Etki Değeri Ekleme Kılavuzu*) · language evidence |
| Evaluation (§5) | Pre-screen on uploaded documents only (web links are ignored), then panelists/advisors: **Araştırma Önerisinin Bilimsel Niteliği 40% · Başvuru Sahibinin Bilimsel Yetkinliği 20% · Araştırmanın Yurt Dışında İlgili Kurumda Yapılma Gerekçesi ve Ev Sahibi Kurumun/Danışmanın Bilimsel Yetkinliği 25% · Kariyer Geliştirme Potansiyeli 15%**. GYK sets the support threshold; separate thresholds may be set for critical-technology / priority topics and for applicants with **≥ 8 Tablo 1 points** from the application year and the two previous calendar years (WoS/Scopus article 2; TR Dizin article 1; WoS BKCI/Scopus book 2; book chapter 1, max 4; CPCI paper 1). First-time beneficiaries may be prioritised; balanced distribution by field may be applied |
| Reporting & obligations | Start the fellowship within **12 months** of the decision (one GYK postponement of up to 6 months) · submit a *kefalet senedi* (with the guarantor's income document) or a *yüklenme senedi + teminat mektubu*, certified by a notary or a Turkish consulate (letter: 30% above the total support, valid 18 months beyond the research period) · host-signed **başlama yazısı** in e-BİDEB within 1 month of departure · **gelişme raporu** by the end of month 6 if the award is ≥ 9 months · **sonuç raporu** + host's *Report of the Host* within 2 months of finishing · return to Türkiye within 12 months of the stipend end and upload the *Yurda Giriş/Çıkış Belgesi* and a job-start letter · **mecburi hizmet** equal to the stipend period · days in Türkiye without deduction: 10 (award ≤ 6 months) or 20 (> 6 months) · no other TÜBİTAK stipend or PTİ at the same time, and not together with a YÖK postdoc-abroad bursary · report outputs and acknowledge TÜBİTAK support |

## 2218 — Yurt İçi Doktora Sonrası Araştırma Burs Programı

Postdoctoral research project **in Türkiye**, at an institution other than the applicant's
doctorate-granting institution or employer.

| Item | Verified content (2026 call) |
|------|------------------------------|
| Purpose (page) | Postdoctoral research projects at Turkish higher-education institutions, research infrastructures, public research institutes, R&D and design centres and technopark companies, to raise productivity and strengthen national collaboration |
| Who can apply (call §4.1) | T.C. citizen or Mavi Kart holder · apply **within 7 years** of the doctorate / specialty / sanatta yeterlik degree (one year added per birth for women researchers, with documentation) — or receive the degree within 12 months of the award date · host is a Turkish state/foundation university, a 6550 research infrastructure, a public research institute, or a company with an R&D/design centre or in a technopark · not at the institution where the applicant is *kadrolu* · doctorate not from the host · invited by the host's highest official · employer permission if employed · no ongoing TÜBİMER objection from the previous period · **≥ 5 Tablo 1 points as of the application date** · an advisor with **≥ 5 Tablo 1 points in the last 2 years** (waived for advisors at TÜBİTAK centres, R&D/design-centre companies, technopark companies and 6550 infrastructures); ≤ 2 advisors, not the PhD advisor, first advisor from the host · ÖSYM language score ≥ **70** (or equivalent) or education at a 100%-foreign-language university · TÜBİTAK staff cannot apply |

| Documents | Research proposal on the 2026 form (see `form_structures.md`) + EK-1 Kaynakça, EK-2 Danışmanlık Planı, EK-3 host invitation letter (topic, advisor, position, permission to use the infrastructure; signed by the host's highest official), EK-4 employer permission if employed. Documents in Turkish or English |
| Evaluation | Pre-screen (a one-week fix window may be given), then panelists/advisors: **Bilimsel Nitelik 40% · Başvuru Sahibinin Bilimsel Yetkinliği 20% · Araştırmanın İlgili Kurumda Yapılma Gerekçesi ve Ev Sahibi Kurumun/Danışmanın Bilimsel Yetkinliği 20% · Kariyer Geliştirme Potansiyeli 20%** |
| Reporting & obligations | Start within 12 months of the decision (GYK may extend by up to 6 months); request the start in PTS ≥ 1 month ahead · **gelişme raporu** for awards > 6 months at TÜBİTAK's dates (upload within 10 days; payments stop if late) · **sonuç raporu** within 2 months of the end · notify paid employment, other funding, trips abroad > 5 working days and contact changes within 10 days · extensions ≤ 50% of the awarded period · suspension up to 12 months for accepted reasons · acknowledge TÜBİTAK in outputs · ethics approval where the study needs it |

## 2221 — Konuk veya Akademik İzinli (Sabbatical) Bilim İnsanı Destekleme Programı

Brings a doctorate-holding scientist **working abroad** to a Turkish host, or supports a
sabbatical spent in Türkiye. **The host-side scientist applies**, not the guest.

| Item | Verified content (2026 call / page) |
|------|-------------------------------------|
| Purpose | Research, lab work, product development, organising conferences, seminars, part-time teaching, joint proposal writing and similar academic/R&D activity by visiting scientists; sabbaticals in Türkiye |
| Who applies | The inviting scientist: *kadrolu* at the Turkish host, holding at least a doctorate, applying with **e-imza** in TYBS; one application per guest |
| Guest conditions | Doctorate + **≥ 4 years** in a job abroad requiring a doctorate (university, research centre or private sector) as of the application date · invited for **7 days – 12 months** (short visits 7 days – 3 months; long visits and sabbaticals 3 – 12 months) · not supported for more than 12 months in total in the last 3 years · at most 2 invitations a year, ≤ 12 months in total · ARBİS record whose name matches the passport |
| What it pays (as of 01.05.2025) | Round-trip travel · **guest 66,000 TL/month; sabbatical 84,000 TL/month** (stays under one month paid per day at the monthly rate ÷ 30) · health insurance **≤ 560 TL/month** for 3–12-month visits. **No research costs** |
| Calendar 2026 | Four periods: **2 Feb – 2 Mar · 4 May – 8 Jun (extended) · 3 Aug – 31 Aug · 2 Nov – 30 Nov 2026**. Apply at the latest in the period before the guest's arrival |
| Documents | ≤ 1 month: *çalışma planı* (Turkish, TÜBİTAK format), guest's doctorate diploma, signed *Letter of Commitment*. > 1 month: *araştırma önerisi* (Turkish, TÜBİTAK format), diploma, Turkish invitation letter signed by an institutional official (not the inviter), leave letter from the guest's employer (a sabbatical letter must say "sabbatical" or "academic leave", otherwise the guest rate applies), *Letter of Commitment* |
| Evaluation | ≤ 1 month: Konuk ve Davet Eden Bilim İnsanının Bilimsel Yetkinliği 40% · Etkinliğin Bilimsel Niteliği 20% · Etkinliğin Yönetimi 20% · Etkinliğin Ev Sahibi Kuruma ve Türkiye'ye Katkısı 15% · Etkinliğin Yöntemi 5%. > 1 month: Yetkinlik 40% · Araştırmanın Bilimsel Niteliği 15% · Yöntemi 15% · Yönetimi 15% · Ev Sahibi Kuruma ve Türkiye'ye Katkısı 15% |
| Reporting & obligations | Guest must start within **6 months** of the decision (GYK may extend by 6 months) · ≤ 1 month: *Report of the Visiting Scientist*, *Değerlendirme Raporu* (+ *Para Teslim Tutanağı* if paid through the inviter) · > 1 month: RVS, **gelişme raporu** at the end of month 6 for long awards (the page says both "6 ay ve üzeri" and "6 aydan fazla" — confirm), **sonuç raporu** within 2 months of the end · outputs reported; TÜBİTAK support and logo acknowledged · the inviter is liable for repayment if the support is cancelled |

Sources: programme page
`https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/burslar/doktora-sonrasi/arastirma-burs-programlari/2221-konuk-veya-akademik-izinli-sabbatical-bilim-insani-destekleme-p`;
2026 call `https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-04/2221_2026_yili_cagri_duyurusu.pdf`;
long-stay form `https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-07/2221_arastirma_onerisi.docx`
(all retrieved 2026-09-23).

## Combining BİDEB and ARDEB support

From the *ARDEB-BİDEB Programlarına Eş Zamanlı Olarak Başvuru Yapabilme Koşulları* table
linked from the 2219 page (`https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-07/ardeb_bideb_es_zamanli_basvuru_yapabilme_kosullari_0.pdf`,
retrieved 2026-09-23):

- **2218, 2232, 2236 (and other listed codes):** applicants, awardees and team members may be PI,
  researcher or advisor on ARDEB projects if eligible; if the ARDEB project is funded, PTİ is not
  paid for the months a BİDEB stipend is paid.
- **2219 (with 2213, 2214):** may apply to ARDEB as PI, and ARDEB applicants may apply to 2219.
  If both are awarded, the table allows both to start when the ARDEB project can be run from
