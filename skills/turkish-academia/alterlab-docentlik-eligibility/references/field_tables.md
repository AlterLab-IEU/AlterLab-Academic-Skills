## Common rules (identical in all 12 tables)

- **Total and post-doctorate points.** Every table requires "asgari yüz (100) puan", of
  which "en az doksan (90) puan" from work after the doctorate, "“3. Lisansüstü Tezlerden
  Üretilmiş Yayın” başlığından alınacak puanlar hariç". The doctorate is "doktora" except in
  Güzel Sanatlar ("doktora/sanatta yeterlik") and Sağlık ("doktora veya tıpta, diş
  hekimliğinde, eczacılıkta ve veteriner hekimlikte uzmanlık").
- **Thesis-derived work.** "Lisansüstü tezlerden üretilen yayınlar, “Lisansüstü Tezlerden
  Üretilmiş Yayın” şartı dışında ayrıca puanlanamaz." Each work is scored in only one
  section (TABLO 11: "Her çalışma, Tablo 11’de sadece bir bölümde puanlandırılır."; TABLO 6
  says "kategoride" instead of "bölümde").
- **Item 3 (thesis-derived) minimum and cap.** "Bu maddenin a-h bentleri kapsamında en az
  bir yayın zorunludur." "Bu maddeden en fazla 20 puan alınabilir."
- **Item 9 (teaching).** "Bu madde kapsamında en az 2 puan almak zorunludur." "Bu maddeden
  en fazla 6 puan alınabilir." Two years as kadrolu öğretim elemanı after the doctorate
  counts as 2 points.
- **Foreign nationals / foreign-doçentlik equivalence.** "Yabancı uyruklu adaylar ile
  yurtdışı doçentlik denkliği başvurusu yapan adaylar, TR Dizin tarafından taranan ulusal
  hakemli dergilerde yayımlanmış makale koşulunu sağlayamamaları durumunda, bunun yerine
  aynı sayıdaki yayını 1. maddenin a, b veya c bentleri kapsamında yapmış olmaları şartı
  aranacaktır."
- **Patents.** "Patentlerde puan kişi sayısına bölünür." No cap is stated for item 10 in
  any table.
- **ÜAK S.S.S. (2026 Mart), same source page.** Q19: the başlıca-yazar definition differs
  per temel alan. Q20: articles in yağmacı/şaibeli journals (YÖK decision 2021.18.643: Q4
  journals charging editorial or article-processing fees, bar stated exceptions) cannot be
  used in the beyanname. Q21: DergiPark hosting is not TR Dizin indexing. Q23: a journal's
  index status at the article's publication date counts. Q24–25: each work is scored in one
  section; citations to it are scored separately under Atıf. Q27: citation points are not
  divided by the author count. Q29: pre-doctorate work counts in its category but not toward
  the 90. Q33: oral-paper points are divided equally among all authors.

## Items 1–2 point rows (the scorer's index tiers)

Identical in all 12 tables (checked row by row on 2026-09-23) except where marked.

| Sub-item | TABLO row (abridged) | Points | Tier code |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1a | SCIE or SSCI journal article, WoS JIF quartile Q1 / Q2 / Q3 / Q4 | 30 / 20 / 15 / 10 | `Q1` `Q2` `Q3` `Q4` |
| 1b | AHCI journal article | 20 | `AHCI` |
| 1c | ESCI or Scopus journal article (Spor: "ESCI, Scopus veya SPORT Discus") | 10 | `ESCI` `Scopus` (`SPORTDiscus`, Spor only) |

kaydıyla" (not derived from the candidate's own thesis). No table caps item 1 or item 2.

## Items 3–13 common point values

For `other_items`, compute your share by hand from these values; the per-field sections list
every value that differs. 3: a) SCIE/SSCI/AHCI article 20, b) ESCI/Scopus 10, c) other
international index 5, d) TR Dizin 8, e) BKCI book 20, f) BKCI chapter 10, g) other book 5,
h) other chapter 3, ı) CPCI-published meeting paper 3, i) other meeting paper 2. 4: a) BKCI
book 20, b) BKCI chapter 10, c) other book 5, d) other chapter 3. 5 (per citing work): a)
SCIE/SSCI/AHCI/ESCI/Scopus 3, b) BKCI book 2, c) TR Dizin journal 2, d) other 1. 6: completed
doctoral thesis supervised 5, master's 3 (a second advisor gets half). 7: a) completed EU
Framework/TÜBİTAK project — coordinator/PI 15, researcher 10, consultant 5; b) international
project 10; c) R&D project with a public/private body outside universities 5; d) BAP project
as PI 3. 8: a) international meeting paper published in CPCI 5, b) other meeting paper 3. 9:
teaching in four different semesters 2, or in two different years 2. 10: international patent
20, national patent 10, utility model 5, personal patent application 2. 11: YÖK / TÜBİTAK /
TÜBA awards 25 each. 12: editor of a SCIE/SSCI/AHCI/ESCI/Scopus journal 2, of a BKCI/Scopus
book 1, of a TR Dizin journal 1. 13 Diğer (where item 13 is "Diğer"; item 14 in Mimarlık,
İlahiyat, Spor): WoS h-index ≥ 5 — 5; ≥ 6 months' uninterrupted research/teaching abroad at a
top-300 university in a YÖK-recognised ranking — 5.
