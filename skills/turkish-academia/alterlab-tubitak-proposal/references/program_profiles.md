## 1002-A — Hızlı Destek Modülü (Fast Support Module)

| Item | Value | As of | Source |
|------|-------|-------|--------|
| Project duration | **≤ 12 months**; no work package may run longer than the project | current | program page + bilgi notu |
| Budget upper limit | **150,000 TRY per year, burs dahil**; no PTİ is paid to yürütücü/araştırmacı/danışman | from 2026-02-01 | program page |
| Application window | **Rolling** — "Başvurularda herhangi bir zaman kısıtlaması yoktur. Yıl boyunca başvuru yapılabilir." e-imza within 15 days of approving the application | current | program page |
| Form | **No .doc template any more.** The content is typed into the PBS screens and the system generates the form, EK-1 and EK-2, following the *1002-A Hızlı Destek Modülü Başvuru İçeriği Bilgi Notu* (per-section word ranges — see `form_structure.md`) | bilgi notu dated 2025-12 | program page + bilgi notu |
| Language | Turkish (English summary and international references excepted); foreign nationals may fill it in English | current | program page |
| Scope | Short-term, small-budget research needs at universities, research hospitals and institutes, **including needs arising in a doctoral thesis**; complementary requests, equipment repair, field work abroad, and infrastructure projects are excluded | current | program page |
| PI (Yürütücü) | University / training-hospital staff with a doctorate; research-institute staff with ≥ 4-year degree; **doktora/uzmanlık/sanatta yeterlik students who passed the qualifying stage**, applying for their accepted thesis's needs (their thesis advisor may be danışman, not araştırmacı) | current | program page |
| Team limits | Across 1002-A and 1002-B at once: 2 as PI, or 1 as PI + 2 as researcher, or 4 as researcher; no researchers or advisors abroad | current | program page |
| Resubmission limit | Not a 3rd time after two insufficient özgün değer scores; not a 4th time after three 1002-A rejections | current | program page |
| Evaluation | External advisors (dış danışman) using the 2025 evaluation form: **Bilimsel Nitelik, Proje Yönetimi, Çıktı-Etki-Kazanımlar** | 2025 form | evaluation form |

## Verification recipe (run before any submission)

1. WebFetch the program page (links above) → confirm duration + budget cap for the **current**
   period, and (1001) the current call dates.
2. For 1001, open the current .doc form and confirm the page/font rules in its header; for
   1002-A, open the current *Başvuru İçeriği Bilgi Notu* and confirm the word ranges.
3. Confirm the **PBS** call is open (1001) or that rolling submission is active (1002-A).
4. Confirm the **ARBİS** record is current (personal, education, experience, expertise keywords,
   publications) before starting PBS entry, and that every signatory has a qualified e-signature.
