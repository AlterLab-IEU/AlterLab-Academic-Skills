|------|-------|-------|--------|

| Application window | **Rolling** — "Başvurularda herhangi bir zaman kısıtlaması yoktur. Yıl boyunca başvuru yapılabilir." e-imza within 15 days of approving the application | current | program page |

yeni başlayan doktoralı bilim insanlarının çalışmalarını proje desteği vererek".

| Item | Value | As of | Source |
|------|-------|-------|--------|

### 3501 vs 1001 — what changes

| Aspect | 1001 | 3501 |
|--------|------|------|
| Who | Doctorate + kadrolu/tam zamanlı; the 1001 page sets no time-since-doctorate or title limit | Early career: ≤ 7 years after the doctorate (+1 year per birth), doçent or lower, first 3501 |
| Budget cap | 3,000,000 TL (2026-1 period) | 1,500,000 TL (from 1 Feb 2026); infrastructure projects excluded |
| Duration | ≤ 36 months | ≤ 36 months |
| Calls | Two calls a year | Rolling, year-round |
| Form | Uploaded .doc, ≤ 25 pages, Arial 9 | PBS text fields with word ranges; system-generated form |
| Extra content | — | *Proje Yürütücüsünün Tez Bilgileri* and **4. Kariyer Geliştirme Potansiyeli** |
| Evaluation | Panel; özgün değer 35 · yöntem 25 · proje yönetimi 20 · yaygın etki 20 | Five criteria incl. Kariyer Geliştirme Potansiyeli, six-level scale, no published weights |
| Team | No professor restriction on the 1001 page; TÜBİTAK centre/institute staff may take part | Professors cannot be researchers; TÜBİTAK centre/institute staff cannot take part in any role |

## Verification recipe (run before any submission)

1. WebFetch the program page (links above) → confirm duration + budget cap for the **current**
   period, and (1001) the current call dates.
2. For 1001, open the current .doc form and confirm the page/font rules in its header; for
   1002-A and 3501, open the current *Başvuru İçeriği Bilgi Notu* and confirm the word ranges.
3. Confirm the **PBS** call is open (1001) or that rolling submission is active (1002-A, 3501);
   for 3501 also confirm the doctorate-date cut-off for the current year.
4. Confirm the **ARBİS** record is current (personal, education, experience, expertise keywords,
   publications) before starting PBS entry, and that every signatory has a qualified e-signature.
