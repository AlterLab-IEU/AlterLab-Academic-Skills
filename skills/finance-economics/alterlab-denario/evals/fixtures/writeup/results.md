# Results (fictional study, eval fixture)

| Width (nm) | κ pristine (W m^-1 K^-1) | κ rough edges (W m^-1 K^-1) |
|---|---|---|
| 1.5 | 412 ± 31 | 192 ± 22 |
| 3.0 | 655 ± 40 | 480 ± 31 |
| 4.5 | 803 ± 44 | 660 ± 37 |
| 6.0 | 891 ± 47 | 772 ± 41 |

κ rises with width for both edge types and has not saturated at 6 nm. Edge roughness lowers κ
by 53% at 1.5 nm and by 13% at 6.0 nm, consistent with boundary scattering that weakens as the
edge-to-area ratio falls. A fit κ_rough = κ_pristine (1 - a/W) gives a = 0.80 ± 0.03 nm.
Figure 1 (plots/kappa_vs_width.png) shows both series with error bars.
