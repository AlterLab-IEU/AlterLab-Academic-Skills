# Study protocol (draft v0.3)

**Working title:** Social media use causes loneliness among university students

**Research question:** Does daily time on social media increase loneliness in undergraduates?

**Design:** One-wave online survey, fielded once in November. No experimental manipulation, no
second wave, no comparison group defined in advance.

**Sample:** Link shared in two course WhatsApp groups and on the department's Instagram account;
we expect about 210 responses and will stop when the semester ends. No sample-size calculation.

**Measures:**
- Loneliness: UCLA Loneliness Scale, 20 items, Turkish adaptation. We will report Cronbach's alpha.
- Social media use: one self-report item, "On a typical day, how many hours do you spend on social media?"
- Covariates: age, gender, year of study.

**Analysis plan:** OLS regression of the loneliness total score on hours of social media use with the
three covariates.

**Planned claim (abstract draft):** "Each additional hour of social media use causes a 2.1-point
increase in loneliness, so universities should limit students' social media time."
