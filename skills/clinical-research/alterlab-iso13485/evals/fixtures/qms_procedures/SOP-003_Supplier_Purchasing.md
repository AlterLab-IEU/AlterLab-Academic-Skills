# SOP-003 Purchasing and Supplier Control

Lumen Microfluidics Inc. (fictional example company) — Rev A, effective 2026-04-15
Owner: Operations Manager

## 1. Purpose
Ensures that purchased product and services conform to specified requirements.

## 2. Procedure
2.1 Approved Supplier List (ASL): a supplier is added after a questionnaire and, for
    critical suppliers (chip molding, reagent fill), an on-site assessment.
2.2 Purchasing information on each PO states the specification, revision and any
    quality agreement.
2.3 Supplier performance is reviewed once a year on on-time delivery and quality.
2.4 Verification of purchased product: goods-in staff carry out incoming inspection
    against the specification before the parts are moved to stock.

## 3. References
ISO 13485:2016 clauses 7.4.1 to 7.4.3
