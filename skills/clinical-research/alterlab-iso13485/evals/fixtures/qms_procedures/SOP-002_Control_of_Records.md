# SOP-002 Control of Records

Lumen Microfluidics Inc. (fictional example company) — Rev A, effective 2026-03-02
Owner: Quality Lead

## 1. Purpose
Keeps quality records legible, identifiable and retrievable.

## 2. Procedure
2.1 Quality records are kept in the eQMS or, for paper originals, in the locked archive
    cabinet in room 104.
2.2 Record retention: at least the lifetime of the device as defined by the company, and
    never less than two years from the date of product release by the company.
2.3 Record retrieval: any record must be retrievable within one working day.
2.4 Confidential health information in records is protected according to applicable law.
2.5 Changes to a record must remain identifiable (no overwriting; strike-through, initial
    and date on paper).

## 3. References
ISO 13485:2016 clause 4.2.5; SOP-001
