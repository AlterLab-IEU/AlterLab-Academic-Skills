# SOP-004 Training and Competence

Lumen Microfluidics Inc. (fictional example company) — Rev A, effective 2026-04-15
Owner: Head of People

## 1. Purpose
Ensures personnel doing work affecting product quality are competent.

## 2. Procedure
2.1 Each role has a competence profile listing required education, skills and experience.
2.2 New starters complete induction training and read-and-understand sign-off for every
    SOP listed on their role matrix before working unsupervised.
2.3 Effectiveness of training is checked by the supervisor through observed practice.
2.4 Training records are kept in the HR system.

## 3. References
ISO 13485:2016 clause 6.2
