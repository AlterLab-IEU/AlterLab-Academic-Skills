# SOP-001 Document Control

Lumen Microfluidics Inc. (fictional example company) — Rev B, effective 2026-03-02
Owner: Quality Lead

## 1. Purpose
Defines how QMS documents are written, reviewed, approved, issued, changed and withdrawn.

## 2. Scope
All controlled documents: SOPs, work instructions, forms, specifications and drawings.

## 3. Procedure
3.1 Numbering: SOP-nnn, WI-nnn, FRM-nnn, DWG-nnn.
3.2 Document approval: the author drafts in the eQMS; the Quality Lead and the area owner
    approve before issue. Approval signatures are electronic.
3.3 Document revision: changes are raised on FRM-001 Change Request, stating the reason and
    the affected documents. Revision letters advance A, B, C ...
3.4 Distribution: only the current revision is available at points of use.
3.5 Obsolete documents are withdrawn from use, watermarked OBSOLETE and kept in the
    archive folder.
3.6 External documents (standards, customer specifications) are listed on FRM-002 and
    checked for currency once a year.

## 4. References
ISO 13485:2016 clause 4.2.4
