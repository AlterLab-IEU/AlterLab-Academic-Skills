# SOP-005 Customer Complaint Handling

Lumen Microfluidics Inc. (fictional example company) — DRAFT, not yet approved
Owner: Quality Lead

## 1. Purpose
Receiving, recording and investigating customer complaints.

## 2. Procedure
2.1 Any employee who receives a complaint logs it on FRM-020 within one working day.
2.2 The Quality Lead decides whether a complaint investigation is required and records
    the reason if not.
2.3 Complaints that may be reportable to a regulatory authority are escalated the same day.
2.4 Complaint files are closed by the Quality Lead.
