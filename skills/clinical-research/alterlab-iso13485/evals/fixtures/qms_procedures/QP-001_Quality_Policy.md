# QP-001 Quality Policy

Lumen Microfluidics Inc. (fictional example company) — Rev A, approved by the CEO 2026-02-10

Lumen Microfluidics designs and makes microfluidic sample-preparation cartridges for
clinical laboratories. We commit to:

- meeting customer and applicable regulatory requirements;
- maintaining the effectiveness of our quality management system;
- giving every employee the knowledge they need to do their job well.

This policy is communicated at induction and displayed in the lab and the office.
