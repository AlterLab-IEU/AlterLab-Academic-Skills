# Halcyon Marine Genomics Lab — milestones 2010–2025

(Fictional lab. Approved for the public homepage by the PI, 2026-09-30.)

Lab name as it should appear: **Halcyon Marine Genomics Lab**
Tagline: *Reading the sea's DNA since 2010*
Brand colours: deep navy #1B2A4A, sea teal #2A9D8F, sand #E9C46A

| Year | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| 2010 | Lab founded at the Aegean Institute of Marine Science with 3 members |
| 2012 | First seagrass genome assembly published |
| 2014 | €1.2M national research council grant for coastal eDNA monitoring |
| 2016 | Karaburun coastal field station opens |
| 2018 | 50th peer-reviewed publication |
| 2019 | Launch of SeaTrace, our open eDNA monitoring database |
| 2021 | 12 autonomous eDNA samplers deployed along the Aegean coast |
| 2023 | Spin-off company Halcyon Bio founded |
| 2025 | 15th anniversary: 28 team members, 140 publications |
