# Decision letter — Computers & Education (manuscript CAE-D-26-01187)

Decision: Major revision

## Editor
The reviewers see promise but agree the causal language outruns the design. Please address
every point and submit a response letter.

## Reviewer 1
1. The study compares two sections taught by different instructors. The paper says AI feedback
   "improved" revision quality; with this design that is an association, not an effect. Either
   reframe or add an analysis that adjusts for section.
2. Inter-rater reliability for the revision-quality rubric is reported only as "high". Report
   the coefficient (and its CI) and how disagreements were resolved.
3. Table 3 reports p-values without effect sizes or confidence intervals.
4. The literature review omits recent work on automated writing evaluation (e.g., studies
   after 2023). Please update.
5. Minor: Figure 2's y-axis is unlabeled.

## Reviewer 2
1. I am not convinced the rubric measures revision quality rather than surface editing. Please
   provide validity evidence or a sample of scored revisions in an appendix.
2. The discussion claims the tool "reduces instructor workload", but workload was not measured.
   Remove or support this claim.
3. Please consider a full randomized trial across all sections of the course. (I realize this
   may be beyond a revision.)
4. Reviewer 1 asks for adjustment by section; I would instead drop the causal framing entirely,
   since two sections cannot support a section-level adjustment.
5. Minor: several references are missing DOIs; the reference list mixes APA 6 and APA 7.
