# Feedback timing and revision quality (draft, APA 7)

Feedback is among the strongest influences on learning (Hattie & Timperley, 2007), although its
effects vary widely and a third of feedback interventions reduce performance (Kluger & DeNisi,
1996). A later meta-analysis confirmed a medium overall effect with large heterogeneity
(Wisniewski et al., 2020). Note-taking research shows that the medium of an activity can change
what students process (Mueller & Oppenheimer, 2014), which motivates comparing feedback delivered
in different modes (Hattie & Timperley, 2007; Wisniewski et al., 2020).
