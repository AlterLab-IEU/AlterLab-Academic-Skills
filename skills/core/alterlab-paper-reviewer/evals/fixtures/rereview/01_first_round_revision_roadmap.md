# First-round review — Revision Roadmap (Major Revision)

*Fictional review written for a review exercise.*

Manuscript: "Peer Mentoring and First-Year Retention Intention in Engineering Faculties"
Decision (round 1): Major Revision

## Priority 1 — Required revisions

- [ ] R1: The engagement scale is used without reliability or validity evidence. Report
  Cronbach's alpha (or omega) for this sample and the factor structure. (Reviewer 1)
- [ ] R2: The design is cross-sectional, yet the abstract and discussion say mentoring
  "increases" retention. Rewrite every causal claim as an association, or justify it with a
  design that can support it. (Reviewer 1, Devil's Advocate)
- [ ] R3: Tables 2 and 3 report only p-values. Add effect sizes with 95% confidence intervals
  for every comparison. (Reviewer 1)

## Priority 2 — Suggested revisions

- [ ] S1: 44 of 412 respondents were dropped for missing items. Explain the missing-data
  pattern and how it was handled. (Reviewer 1)
- [ ] S2: The literature review stops in 2020; add recent work on peer mentoring in STEM.
  (Reviewer 2)
